I've had my own computer since I was 12, and I've been rocking Windows ever since Windows XP, where I played plenty of games\
Windows 7 became my main OS, since only the unknowing ones tried Vista, and my knowledge really ramped up in the Windows 10 era
> The good old days, when people used Hamachi, not knowing how much access they gave everyone else on the same network

Since 2018 I've also used Ubuntu for anything server related, and dug deeper into it over the years

Minecraft, Garry's Mod and Portal 2 taught me permission systems, plugins, modding, servers and problem solving long before I knew those were real skills
> Portal 2 also taught me that any surface, no matter the distance, might be a way out

I taught myself AfterEffects and PhotoShop the same way, plus some Blender and Cinema4D\
So I'm as much creative as I am technical, and happiest when a project needs both

My download speed is great, so I can absorb massive amounts of information and go deep when something matters\
Upload on demand is moodier for various reasons, and while it's all in there, it doesn't always come out when asked\
I also tend to map the edge cases other people skip, and I think every path is worth understanding, filtered through the practical question of whether the resources fit the goal
